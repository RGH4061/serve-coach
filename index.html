How to film a good clip

Put your phone on a tripod or fence, side-on, 4–6 metres away. Keep your whole body and the top of the toss in frame. Use slow motion (120 or 240 fps) if you can, and a plain background behind the toss so the ball is easy to spot.

If an iPhone video won't load, set Settings › Camera › Formats to "Most Compatible", or use Safari.
