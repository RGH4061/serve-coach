1. Film your serve

Phone on a tripod or fence, side-on to you, about 4–6 metres away. Keep your whole body and the top of the toss in shot. Use slow-motion (120 or 240 fps) if you can, and keep the clip short: one serve, from setting up to landing.

If an iPhone video won't load, set Settings › Camera › Formats to "Most Compatible", or open the page in Safari.
